Privacy Policy

Planet Escapes · Effective October 8, 2026

In short

  • Planet Escapes does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and has no accounts of its own.
  • It shows no advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your progress (coins, level, upgrades, weapons, best scores, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • Co-op play, leaderboards and purchases are handled by Apple (Game Center and the App Store).

Information stored on your device

The game saves your coins, level and experience, upgrades, owned weapons and call-ins, best scores, your last planet (so you can continue) and settings on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This data is not sent to us.

Co-op and leaderboards (Apple Game Center)

If you play co-op, the game uses Apple's Game Center to sign you in and match you with other players. During a match, gameplay information (such as your position, aim and the creatures on the planet) is sent between the players' devices through Apple's servers, and your Game Center nickname is shown to the other players. Your scores are posted to Game Center leaderboards, where other players can see them with your nickname. We do not receive your Apple Account details. Apple's handling of this data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (App Store)

Coin packs are bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the game only receives confirmation of what was bought so it can deliver it. We never see your payment details.

Technical data from the game engine

Planet Escapes is made with the Unity engine. Unity's in-app purchase system used by the game may handle limited technical information, for example device model and operating system version, to make purchases work. The game does not use Unity's analytics or advertising services. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Contacting us

If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
